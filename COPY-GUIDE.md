# Tenacore website copy

Core message: One place for all your digital needs.

Explain the offer immediately: websites and apps, business software, and IT support. Use branding and marketing as part of the wider service range.

Write for business owners and teams without a technical background. Use concrete actions and familiar words. Explain technical terms in context. Each page should answer: what is offered, how it helps, and what to do next.

Use short sentences and descriptive headings. Avoid slogans that need interpretation, unsupported promises, invented results, and describing Tenacore as only a design agency. Project pages describe the visible design work without claiming unverified commercial outcomes.

Contact form copy must accurately explain that the current form opens an email draft.
