A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Have a question before you start?

Here are a few things you might want to know.

Ask us a question
Can you help if I’m not sure what I need?

Yes. Tell us what is happening in your business and what you want to improve. We’ll explain the options in plain language.

Can you help with more than one service?

Yes. For example, we can discuss your website, billing software, and network together. We’ll agree on the scope for each part.

Do you provide support after setup?

We’ll discuss the support you need and agree on the scope before starting. The arrangement depends on the service or product.

How do I get a price?

Contact us with a few details. We’ll discuss the work, timeline, and requirements before preparing a quote.
